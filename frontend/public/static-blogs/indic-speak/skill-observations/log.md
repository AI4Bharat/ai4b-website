## 2026-09-04

### Observation 1: Prototype narrative motion before polishing
**Status:** OPEN
**Skill:** New skill candidate: editorial interaction design
**Category:** workflow
**Proprietary:** false
**Confidence:** high

**Context:** A blog hero animation was visually polished and technically sound, but successive user feedback showed that it still felt unsatisfying because its dominant motion was decorative rather than explanatory.

**Issue:** Static screenshots and implementation checks can validate composition, responsiveness, and mechanics without validating whether an animation communicates the article's central idea. Refining an unproven visual metaphor compounds work without resolving the conceptual mismatch.

**Suggested improvement:** For editorial motion work, define the one-sentence story the animation must tell, prototype its three key states (input, transformation, outcome), and validate that sequence before investing in texture, easing, or ambient effects. Review both a still frame and the actual timed transition.

**Principle:** Validate the narrative function of motion before polishing its visual execution; an animation earns prominence by explaining the subject, not merely by adding movement.
