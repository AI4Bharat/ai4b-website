Ripple, four ways

All four are the same idea — sound radiating from a source, driven by Kavya’s real loudness envelope — drawn at full resolution with far fewer and crisper rings than the version on the post. Tell me a letter (or a mix, e.g. “C but slower”) and I will put it on the hero.

AClean rings

Twelve thin rings, wide apart, crisp hairlines. What a ripple actually looks like: you can follow one ring all the way out. Weight and colour still come from the envelope.
